🔥 VTL/OER Calculator

Measure your video's like engagement, overall engagement, and OER Score.
Built for the FireballMC Creator Hub.

📊 Enter Your Video Statistics

Total video views. Must be at least 1.
Total likes. Zero is allowed.
Total comments. Every 2 comments count as 1 weighted like.
Default target: 10%. Used to calculate the score out of 100.

📈 Your Video Results

VTL Ratio
—
Average views per like. Lower is better for like efficiency.
Like Engagement
—
Likes divided by views, multiplied by 100.
Overall Engagement
—
Likes plus half the comments, divided by views.
OER Score
—
Your OER compared with the selected target.
Weighted Engagement
—
Likes + (comments ÷ 2).

OER Target Progress

Calculate your video to see target progress.

VTL is undefined when a video has zero likes. OER Score is capped at 100/100. The default 10% target is a customizable benchmark for this calculator, not a universal industry standard.
